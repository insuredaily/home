The Geometry of Silence

add_photo_alternate Set Featured Image

Exploring how empty space and deliberate lack of ornamentation creates profound emotional resonance in densely populated concrete jungles.

Elena Rostova

Oct 12, 2024 • 12 min read

drag_indicator add_circle

In the heart of the metropolis, where visual noise constantly assaults the senses, a new movement of architectural design is taking root. It doesn't scream for attention with sweeping curves or reflective glass; instead, it whispers through absence.

The concept of 'Ma' in Japanese aesthetics refers to the negative space—the gap, the pause, the distance between things. When applied to modern urban design, this translates to generous plazas devoid of statues, blank concrete walls that serve only to catch the changing shadows of the day.
